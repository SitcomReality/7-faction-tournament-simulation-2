# The Supernal Interregnum: Lore & Setting

## The Godsfall

The age of gods has ended. Not with trumpets or apocalypse, but with a terrible silence. The divine pantheons, locked in eons of cosmic conflict, finally consumed themselves in a war that cracked the firmament itself.

What remains are **Seven Divine Aspects** - the fundamental forces that once composed divinity. Without gods to contain them, these primal essences scatter across the mortal realm like shattered starlight, waiting to be claimed.

## The Shattered Realm

The world is fragmented, both physically and spiritually:

- **Sanctums**: Sacred sites where divine essence pools and crystallizes
- **The Veil**: Regions untouched by divine light, unknowable until consecrated
- **The Between**: Ocean and void that separate islands of sacred power
- **Consecrated Ground**: Territories claimed by Claimant Houses, channels for divine will

## The Claimant Houses

In the power vacuum left by the gods, seven mortal factions have risen, each bearing fragments of divine bloodline or ancient pacts:

1. **House Aurorus** - Last scions of the sun kings
2. **House Nocturn** - Shadow-touched seers of the moon court
3. **House Vortan** - Storm riders of the sky citadels
4. **House Lithic** - Earth-bound keepers of the deep halls
5. **House Ignaar** - Flame-born smiths of the sacred forge
6. **House Pelagos** - Tide callers from drowned kingdoms
7. **House Astral** - Star-touched wanderers between worlds

*Note: Player count may be fewer than seven - some Houses may remain dormant or NPC-controlled*

## The Divine Aspects

Each Aspect represents a primal force with inherent cosmic opposition:

- **Radiance**: The piercing light that banishes shadow but is reflected by water
- **Umbra**: The encompassing darkness that smothers light but is consumed by fire
- **Tempest**: The raging storm that scatters flame but is grounded by earth
- **Terra**: The unyielding stone that withstands tempest but is eroded by tide
- **Pyre**: The eternal fire that burns through shadow but is quenched by water
- **Tide**: The flowing sea that drowns earth but evaporates before radiance
- **Aether**: The infinite void that absorbs all yet disperses when concentrated

These relationships are not merely symbolic - they represent the fundamental laws by which divine power interacts.

## The Path to Ascension

To restore cosmic balance (or seize godhood), a Claimant must:

1. **Consecrate Sanctums** - Claim sacred sites across the realm
2. **Awaken Aspects** - Perform divine rites to unlock dormant powers (costs Essence)
3. **Manifest Divinity** - Deploy Aspects to sanctums, extending control
4. **Navigate Opposition** - Use the cosmic wheel's relationships strategically
5. **Achieve Dominion** - Control sufficient sacred sites OR Awaken all Seven Aspects

## Divine Essence (Resource System)

Every turn, Claimants generate **Essence** from their sanctums:
- 1 Essence per controlled Sanctum
- Essence is spent to:
  - **Awaken** a new Divine Aspect (cost increases: 3/5/7/9 Essence)
  - **Manifest** an Aspect at a Sanctum (1 Essence)
  - Perform special divine rites (varies)

## Victory Conditions

### Ascension (Primary)
Control 50% or more of all Sanctums while having at least 5 Aspects Awakened

### Divine Omniscience (Alternative)
Be the first to Awaken all Seven Divine Aspects

### Last House Standing (Elimination)
All other Claimants are eliminated (control no Sanctums)

## Gameplay Philosophy

This is not a game of armies and logistics - it's a game of **divine chess**. Each Aspect placement is a move in a cosmic puzzle. The 2-paradoxical relationship graph ensures there's no single dominant strategy - every power has its place, every deployment its counter.

Victory comes from:
- Reading the cosmic wheel and anticipating opposition
- Strategic timing of Aspect Awakenings
- Territorial positioning and Sanctum control  
- Resource management (Essence economy)
- Exploiting the Veil to surprise opponents

The goal is to create a game where every turn feels meaningful, every decision has weight, and victory comes from mastery of the Seven Aspects' eternal dance.
